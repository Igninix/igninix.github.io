Igninix

Those who love you will celebrate your arrival in this world by preparing a feast, singing songs, and offering blessings. When you pass away, those who miss you will grieve by holding a funeral and lighting lanterns. In life and in death, there will always be those who are moved by your existence. So take the chance, while your soul is full of life, to come together with your loved ones and be happy.

I don't like the idea of "accompanying" someone on a journey. It sounds romantic at first, but when you think about it, the word "accompany" makes one person the protagonist and the other a mere sidekick.

The truth is, every step we take is our own, as are the things we experience and the ways we grow along the way. So, I won't say that I'm "accompanying" you on your journey — rather that I'm glad to have the pleasure of your company on what happens to be, for now, a shared journey.

— Kyryll Chudomirovich Flins — Vesna Strivosha
